## Open-Source Reference Libraries & Systems
1. **Adaptive Camera Rig**
   - **Repository:** https://github.com/DoctorArta/godot-adaptive-camera-rig
   - **Author:** Arta (DoctorArta)
   - **License:** MIT License
   - **Usage:** Dual SpringArm architecture, shoulder offset switching, camera occlusion prevention, pitch clamping, exponential damping interpolation.

2. **Real Controller**
   - **Repository:** https://github.com/fdemir/real-controller
   - **Author:** fdemir
   - **License:** MIT License
   - **Usage:** CharacterBody3D physics, grounded/aerial acceleration and deceleration equations, coyote time, jump buffering, and responsive 3D third-person controls.

3. **GTA-mini (Night Drop)**
   - **Repository:** https://github.com/alexbieber/GTA-mini
   - **Author:** Alex Bieber & Night Drop contributors
   - **License:** MIT License
   - **Usage:** Open-world city layout inspiration, road circuit topology, vehicle collision/braking architecture, pedestrian AI navigation patterns, volumetric lighting, and graphics environment setup.

4. **Godot Settings Menus**
   - **Repository:** https://github.com/seloc0des/godot-settings-menus
   - **Author:** seloc0des
   - **License:** MIT License
   - **Usage:** Settings structure (Audio, Display, Graphics, Controls, Accessibility), pause menu overlay, focus retention, and keyboard/gamepad navigation flow.

## Original IP & Assets
All superhero models, character abilities, names, visual effects, city layouts, and mission scripts in this project are original creations:
- **Crimson Weaver**: Original agility superhero design built from 3D primitives and custom shaders.
- **Shadow Knight**: Original tactical vigilante design built from 3D primitives and metallic shaders.
- **Coastal City Sandbox**: Procedural city blocks, waterfront, skyscrapers, plazas, and rooftop traversal anchors.
- **Sound & Visual Design**: Engine procedural audio and shader materials.
